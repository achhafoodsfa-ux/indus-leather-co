Our Process

01

Leather Sourcing

Full-grain cow leather selected against a written specification — grade, thickness and tannage agreed with you before production, not substituted after.

02

Hand-Stitching

Cut, stitched and edge-finished by Lahore leather craftsmen. Saddle stitching throughout the flagship range — a broken stitch does not unravel the seam.

03

Quality Checks

Every unit is checked before packing: stitching, edge finish, slot fit with real cards, colour consistency and box presentation. Faulty units are rejected at our cost, not shipped and argued about later.

04

Export Packing & Documents

Export-grade carton packing with a full document set: commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin / statement on origin where applicable, filed through Pakistan's PSW/WEBOC export system via our clearing agent.

Quality & Compliance

We sell leather that touches skin, so we take materials compliance seriously — and we will show you evidence rather than adjectives.

Full-Grain Specification

Our card holders are made from full-grain leather — the complete top layer of the hide. We do not use bonded leather, split leather or PU substitutes in this range, and the material specification is stated in writing on every quotation.

REACH-Aware Materials

UK/EU buyers must meet UK REACH limits for leather in skin contact, including chromium VI below 3 mg/kg (Annex XVII, Entry 47) and restrictions on azo colourants (Entry 43). We source leather with these limits in mind and discuss your market's requirements before production, not after.

Lab Testing Available on Order

Independent accredited laboratory testing (chromium VI and azo, per colour/material) can be arranged with internationally recognised labs operating in Pakistan, with reports issued against the actual production leather for your order. Tell us your compliance file requirements at enquiry stage.

Buyer FAQ

Can I order a sample before committing to a bulk order?

Yes — and we recommend it. Samples are paid (price quoted per design, plus courier at cost), and the sample fee is credited against your first bulk order. Judge the leather, stitching and box yourself before any larger decision.

What is your minimum order quantity?

100 units for the flagship card holder, with price tiers at 300, 500 and 1,000 units. Mixed colours within an order can be discussed at enquiry.

Can you put my logo and branding on the product?

Yes. Blind or foil embossing/debossing on the leather, plus private-label gift boxes and care cards to your artwork. Artwork approval and a pre-production sample are part of the process before bulk production starts.

What are your payment terms?

50% advance by bank transfer (T/T) to confirm the order, and 50% before dispatch against final photographs and packing details. These terms protect both sides on a first trade; terms can be revisited as a trading relationship develops.

Who handles customs, duty and VAT in my country?

You do, as importer of record — our prices are FOB Pakistan. We provide the complete export document set (commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin / statement on origin where applicable). For reference, UK MFN duty on HS 4202 31 00 is 2%, and Pakistan-origin goods may qualify for preferential treatment under the UK's DCTS scheme — your customs broker can confirm what applies to your shipment.

How long will my order take?

Estimates, confirmed in writing for your specific order: samples approx. 2–4 weeks including courier; production approx. 3–5 weeks from deposit and artwork approval. Freight time depends on the method you choose (air or sea) as the importer arranging carriage from Pakistan.

What if units arrive faulty?

Every unit is checked before packing. If a production fault reaches you, tell us with photographs within an agreed window and we will credit or replace the affected units against your next order — the exact remedy is written into the order confirmation so there are no surprises on either side.

Do you offer exclusivity?

Not on a trial order. Once a first order has been paid and delivered, we are open to discussing exclusivity for a named design or channel, for a fixed period and against a minimum purchase quantity — in writing.
